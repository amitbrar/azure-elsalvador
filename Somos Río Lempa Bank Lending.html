Somos Río Lempa Bank Lending

Azure Source Capital · El Salvador

Using Somos Río Lempa funds to grow bank lending for water in the Lempa basin

Banks already lend to water in El Salvador: 85% of ASC’s $13.3 mil disbursed since 2019 (32 of 39 loans)

Banks lent in every year, and to all three kinds of water service provider.

What it will take to increase ASC lending in El Salvador

…technical assistance

To WSPs

  • TA for engineering and project design
  • TA to strengthen revenue streams (metering, setting tariffs), accounting and financial systems
  • TA to improve governance

To banks

  • TA for underwriting water sector loans

…financial incentives to banks

  • Lower cost of financing from ASC (2‑3% lower rates)
  • Disbursement linked incentives (Aceli model)
  • Credit guarantees (as collateral substitute)

…process changes and improvements

  • Lending through ASC subsidiary instead of Fideagua (gives ASC more control over the process, is more tax efficient, and avoids the cupo, the per-bank exposure limit in the Fideagua reglamento)

What will it cost, and who pays for it

CRS pays for technical assistance to water service providers. Somos Río Lempa (SRL) pays for everything else.

Key questions and next steps

  1. Choosing banksWhich banks to start with, for which segments, and what each needs before it says yes.
  2. Designing financial incentivesSize, trigger and payment terms for disbursement linked incentives, tested with banks.
  3. Guarantee mechanismFirst-loss level, who holds the money, cash or promise, and how claims get paid.
  4. Pipeline for municipal and community loansList ready deals and estimate market size in each segment.
  5. Lending from ASC subsidiary to banksAre all regulatory requirements met? Confirm licensing, tax and supervisory rules.
  6. TA needed for banksWhat underwriting support each bank needs, and what it costs.
